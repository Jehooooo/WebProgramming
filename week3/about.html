Our History & Identity

About Union Christian College

Founded in 1910 in the City of San Fernando, La Union, Union Christian College (UCC) is a private Christian educational institution committed to Whole Person Education. For 116 years, UCC has formed learners who combine strong academic preparation with Christian character and community service across the Ilocos Region.

Mission & Vision

Union Christian College strives to develop competent, ethical, and service-oriented graduates guided by Christian faith, quality instruction, relevant research, and active outreach in local and global communities.

  • Whole Person Education: Developing mind, body, and spirit together.
  • Academic Quality: Preparing students for board exams and careers.
  • Community Outreach: Serving churches, families, and neighbors in La Union.

Core Values (F.I.R.E.S.)

  • Faith: Living out Christ-centered teachings in daily life.
  • Integrity: Practicing honesty, fairness, and accountability.
  • Responsibility: Taking ownership of learning and leadership.
  • Excellence: Aiming for high standards in academics and work.
  • Service: Helping others with compassion and humility.

Academic Units

  1. School of Education, Arts and Sciences (SEAS): Teacher Education, Liberal Arts, and Sciences
  2. School of Business Science (SBS): Accountancy, Business Administration, and Management
  3. School of Health Sciences (SHS): BS Nursing and Allied Health Programs
  4. Basic Education Schools (BES): Elementary, Junior High, and Senior High School
  5. Graduate Studies: Master of Arts in Nursing (MAN) and Master of Arts in Education (MAEd)
