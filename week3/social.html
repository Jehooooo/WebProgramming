Online Community Guidelines

  • Be Respectful: Keep comments and discussions courteous and aligned with UCC's core values.
  • Verify Announcements: Always rely on official UCC pages and notices for class suspension or enrollment updates.
  • Protect Privacy: Do not post personal student numbers, grades, or passwords in public comments.
  • Use Official Channels for Support: Send formal inquiries through the Contact page or campus offices.
