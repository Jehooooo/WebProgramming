About Union Christian College

Founded in 1910 in the City of San Fernando, La Union, Union Christian College (UCC) is a private Christian educational institution dedicated to Christ-centered Whole Person Education. For 116 years, UCC has nurtured globally competitive graduates grounded in Christian faith, research innovation, and community service across the Ilocos Region.

Mission & Vision

Union Christian College develops competent, ethical, and service-oriented graduates guided by Christian faith, quality instruction, relevant research, and active community outreach.

  • Whole Person Education: Developing mind, body, and spirit together.
  • Academic Quality: Preparing learners for board exams and global careers.
  • Community Outreach: Serving churches, families, and neighbors in La Union.

Core Values (F.I.R.E.S.)

  • Faith: Living out Christ-centered teachings in daily life since 1910.
  • Integrity: Upholding Christian ethics, truth, and academic honesty.
  • Responsibility: Active stewardship and servant student leadership.
  • Excellence: Consistent top-performing licensure and academic standards.
  • Service: Dedicated outreach to church and community in La Union.

UCC Academic Schools

  1. School of Education, Arts & Sciences (SEAS): Teacher Education, Liberal Arts & Sciences
  2. School of Business Science (SBS): Accountancy, Business Administration & Management
  3. School of Health Sciences (SHS): BS Nursing & Allied Health Programs
  4. Basic Education Schools (BES): Elementary, Junior High & Senior High School
  5. Graduate Studies: Master of Arts in Nursing (MAN) & Education (MAEd)
