About Union Christian College

Founded in 1910 in the City of San Fernando, La Union, Union Christian College (UCC) is dedicated to Christ-centered Whole Person Education. For over 116 years, UCC has nurtured globally competitive graduates grounded in Christian faith, research innovation, and community service across the Ilocos Region.

Mission, Vision & Quick Facts

Union Christian College strives to develop competent, ethical, and service-oriented graduates guided by Christian faith, quality instruction, relevant research, and active outreach in local and global communities.

  • Founded: 1910 (116 Years of Whole Person Education)
  • Address: Widdoes St., Brgy. II, City of San Fernando, La Union 2500
  • Levels Offered: Basic Education, College, and Graduate Studies

UCC Academic Schools & Programs

  1. School of Education, Arts and Sciences (SEAS): Teacher Education, Liberal Arts & Sciences
  2. School of Business Science (SBS): Accountancy, Business Administration & Management
  3. School of Health Sciences (SHS): BS Nursing & Allied Health Programs
  4. Basic Education Schools (BES): Elementary, Junior High & Senior High School
  5. Graduate Studies: Master of Arts in Nursing (MAN) & Master of Arts in Education (MAEd)

Core Values (F.I.R.E.S.) & Campus Highlights

  • Faith: Christ-centered Whole Person Education since 1910
  • Integrity: Upholding Christian ethics, truth, and academic honesty
  • Responsibility: Active stewardship and servant student leadership
  • Excellence: Consistent top-performing board licensure readiness
  • Service: Dedicated outreach to church and community in La Union
